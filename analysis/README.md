# Analysis

Studies that sit outside the two-stage CompIRT pipeline. They fall into two
groups that are kept separate because they answer different kinds of question
and have different outputs.

For the CompIRT models themselves see the model-family directories and
`compirt_eval/`; for the dataset see
[`../docs/dataset-schema.md`](../docs/dataset-schema.md).

## Descriptive studies

These read the built HuggingFace tables under `data/hf/quizbowl-irt/` and write
figures and CSVs *beside their own code*. They fit no CompIRT model and write
nothing under `results/`.

| Folder | Scope | What it answers |
|---|---|---|
| [season_2024_25_eda/](season_2024_25_eda/) | The one complete season: 33 sites, 4 events, Oct 2024 – Apr 2025 | Difficulty as a timing gradient, within-season player progression, selection measured on a fixed question set; complete category metadata |
| [terminal_cell/](terminal_cell/) | Focused study | Empirical team first-buzz hazard cell by cell; why the final token position needs its own intercept |

`season_2024_25_eda/` is the cleanest slice of the data — the only complete
season, and the only one with no missing category metadata. Note that
calendar-year splits cut this season in half.

`terminal_cell/` is the measurement behind the terminal-cell intercepts in the
double-difficulty families — see
[`../docs/double-difficulty-bernstein.md`](../docs/double-difficulty-bernstein.md#terminal-cell-intercepts).

Each folder documents its own reproduction command in its `README.md`.

## Bonus-prediction experiments

[`bonus_team_irt/`](bonus_team_irt/README.md) learns team skill vectors directly
from bonus outcomes, with one- and three-dimensional IRT arms. It compares
frozen whole-game predictions with part-conversion rates and saved
compositional models on matched complete games. Outputs live under
`results/compirt/holdout/common/bonus-team-irt/`; findings are recorded in
[`../RESEARCH_LOG_2.md`](../RESEARCH_LOG_2.md).

## Correctness-prediction experiments

[`correctness/`](correctness/) is a single research line in three packages: given
the observed buzzer and buzz position, was the buzz correct? Unlike the
descriptive studies these *fit models*, use the five whole-game holdout folds,
and write under `results/compirt/holdout/common/`.

Start at [`correctness/README.md`](correctness/README.md) for the shared
protocol, the package-to-results map, and the run order. The consolidated
finding is [`../docs/correctness-benchmarks.md`](../docs/correctness-benchmarks.md)
(generated by `correctness/tossup_accuracy_study/report.py`); the assessment that
started the line is
[`paper-readiness-tossups/REPORT.md`](../results/compirt/holdout/common/paper-readiness-tossups/REPORT.md).
